What is the most logical step for us to take next?

Are we in the middle of a sequence of work that needs to be completed in order to enable a future step? Do we need to change our approach on anything? Has a condition arisen that needs special handling?

Let’s think step by step and check our work to make sure we get the right answer. If this involves a codebase or repository, ground the options and pros/cons in the actual repo state and cite `file:line`; otherwise cite or quote reputable sources, so we know it is grounded.
